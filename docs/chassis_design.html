Modular 3-Tier Chassis Architecture

Tier 1 • Top Level (Height: 80cm – 120cm)

Bottle Inspection Cradle & User Interface

Ergonomically positioned at natural waist-to-chest level for intuitive user operation and clear visibility of the 16×2 instruction screen.

  • Bottle Cradle: 35cm × 12cm horizontal chute with V-groove cradle to centralize bottles automatically.
  • Rigid Cradle Mount: Bolted directly to shelf frame with zero fragile deflection points, eliminating mechanical fatigue.
  • Proximity Probes: LJC18A3 (Capacitive plastic) and LJ12A3 (Inductive metal) mounted 8mm from bottle flank.
  • HC-SR04 Ultrasonic: Mounted at the rear end-stop facing the bottle bottom to measure bottle height (Mismo ~18cm vs 1.5L ~30cm).
  • MG996R Servo Flap: Hinged bottom flap. Tilts 90° down to drop accepted bottle into lower bin, or tilts reverse/pushes forward to reject.
Tier 2 • Mid Level (Height: 45cm – 80cm)

Coin Hopper & Electronics Control Bay

Secure, dry compartment physically isolated from the bottle dropping path to protect sensitive electronics and coin inventory.

  • 12V Coin Hopper: Bolted to mid-shelf with dedicated 45° smooth coin chute feeding down into front retrieval cup.
  • Controller Board: Arduino Uno on DIN-rail or standoff mounts with quick-disconnect terminal blocks.
  • Dual-Rail Power: S-120-12 (12V 10A PSU) + LM2596 buck converter supplying stable 5.0V (logic) and 4.0V (SIM800L).
  • GSM SIM800L Bay: Positioned with external stub antenna away from power transformers to prevent RF noise.
Tier 3 • Base Level (Height: 0cm – 45cm)

High-Capacity Bottle Collection Bin

Spacious, easily serviceable collection chamber designed to store up to 30–50 empty plastic bottles before requiring emptying.

  • Removable Tub: Standard 50-liter plastic tote container with integrated side handles for rapid removal.
  • Full Bin Sensing: Upper IR obstacle beam detects when bottles stack up to the ceiling threshold.
  • Safety Limit: Firmware software counter halts operation at 30 bottles and triggers SMS alert to the technician.
  • Mobility Base: 4× heavy-duty polyurethane caster wheels with dual-locking foot brakes.

Chassis Fabrication & Assembly Sequence

1

Frame Assembly

Cut aluminum extrusions (or plywood frame panels). Assemble the rectangular tower box (120cm × 50cm × 45cm) using corner brackets and M5 T-nuts. Bolt the 4 caster wheels to the bottom base plate.

2

Partition Shelves

Install the two horizontal dividing shelves: Upper shelf (at 80cm height) for the bottle testing cradle and servo flap; Mid shelf (at 45cm height) for the coin hopper and electronics enclosure.

3

Sensor Cradle Rig

Firmly bolt the V-groove bottle cradle to the top shelf. Mount the LJC18 capacitive and LJ12 inductive probes at 8mm distance from the bottle side wall, and the HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor at the rear stop.

4

Servo Trapdoor & Chute

Attach the MG996R metal-gear servo horn to the acrylic trapdoor flap. Verify that when rotating from 0° to 90°, the flap drops the bottle straight into the lower bin without jamming.

5

Coin Hopper & Payout

Firmly mount the 12V Coin Hopper. Fasten an inclined PVC or sheet metal coin ramp leading directly from the hopper exit snout down to the front-panel coin retrieval cup.

6

Front Panel & UI

Mount the 16×2 I2C LCD, the 3 dedicated push buttons (Green 1.5L, Blue Mismo, Red Cancel/Reset), and status LEDs into laser-cut front door bezels. Connect wiring harnesses with Dupont strain relief.
