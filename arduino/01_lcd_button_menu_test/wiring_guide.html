Calibration & Testing Tips
  • LCD Contrast Knob: If nothing appears on the screen when powered, turn the small blue potentiometer on the back of the I2C backpack using a small screwdriver until letters become sharp and visible.
  • Single Button Interaction: Quick click cycles between 1.5L and Mismo. Hold for 1.5 seconds until a longer beep sounds to enter deposit mode!
  • Serial Monitor: Open Arduino Serial Monitor at 115200 baud to view realtime state output.
