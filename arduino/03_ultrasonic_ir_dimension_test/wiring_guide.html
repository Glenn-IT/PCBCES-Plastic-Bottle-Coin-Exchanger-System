This bench setup validates your Vertical Drop Chamber (43 cm physical height from bottom trapdoor to ceiling sensor). The HC-SR04 ultrasonic transceiver is mounted at the top ceiling looking down at the bottle cap. A tall 1.5L / 1.7L bottle (~30–33 cm) leaves a short distance of 7 cm to 15 cm to the ceiling. A shorter Mismo bottle (~16–19 cm) sits lower down, measuring 24 cm to 27 cm to the ceiling. The IR obstacle sensor is mounted on the entry wall to immediately wake up scanning when a bottle is inserted.

Bench Testing & Calibration Procedure
1

Verify 5V Logic

Ensure the HC-SR04+ and IR sensor are connected to the 5V regulated rail and share a common ground with the Arduino Uno.

2

Flush Mount & Calibrate IR Sensor

Mount the IR sensor body outside the chamber with lens flush to the wall (do NOT let the sensor head protrude into the 15°–30° ultrasonic cone). Calibrate the potentiometer screw so the onboard LED lights ONLY when an object is in the entry chute.

3

Upload Sketch

Open 03_ultrasonic_ir_dimension_test.ino in Arduino IDE. Select Tools → Board → Arduino Uno, select your COM port, and click Upload.

4

Open Serial Monitor

Open Serial Monitor and set baud rate to 115200 baud. Place a 1.5L bottle and then a Mismo bottle in the vertical chamber to watch the live classification stream.
