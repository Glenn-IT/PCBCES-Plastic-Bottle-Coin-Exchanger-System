🛡️
Autonomous Protection & Emergency SMS Telemetry Logic
01

Payout Trigger

User deposits bottle quota (or tester sends '1' or '2'). Relay turns ON (Pin D8 LOW), initiating rotation of the coin dispensing disc.

02

Pulse Monitoring & Timer Reset

Every coin passing through the optical slot triggers an active LOW pulse on D7. Each detected pulse increments the coin tally and resets the safety timer to 0 ms.

03

5-Second Timeout Check

If 5000 ms elapses without a single coin pulse (e.g. hopper is empty or coins are jammed in the chute), the firmware instantly halts the motor (Pin D8 HIGH).

04

Automated SMS Dispatch

The Arduino commands the SIM900A module via AT commands to send an urgent SMS: "ALERT: PCBCES Coin Hopper is EMPTY or JAMMED! No coin dispensed for 5 seconds..."

⚠️ Hardware Safety Protection Rationale

High-torque coin hopper motors are designed to operate under intermittent load. Allowing a hopper to spin dry indefinitely when empty will cause motor overheating, plastic gear wear, and unfulfilled payouts. The 5-second cutoff ensures complete mechanical safety while alerting the operator immediately via cellular SMS.
