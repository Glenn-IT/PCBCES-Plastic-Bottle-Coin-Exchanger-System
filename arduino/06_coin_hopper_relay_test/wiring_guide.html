220V AC Mains
Thick Brown (Live) & Blue (Neutral) wires at motor rear. Switched safely via 5V Relay module (COM & NO).
5V Relay Module
10A 250VAC Rated
Songle relay contacts isolate 220V AC from Arduino 5V logic. Active LOW trigger on Pin D8.
Sensor Board Power
+5V DC Regulated
Board: YT LP-08 A01. Pin 1 (VCC) & Pin 3 (GND) connect to Arduino 5V and common ground rail.
Coin Pulse Output
Direct 5V Logic (D7)
Pin 2 (SIG) connects directly to Arduino D7. Active LOW falling edge when a coin breaks the optical beam.

💡 Direct 5V Sensor Interfacing (No Resistor Divider Needed):

Because the `YT LP-08 A01` sensor board is powered by the 5V DC rail (from your Arduino/breadboard), its Pin 2 (SIG) output signal is already clean 5.0V TTL logic! It connects directly into Arduino Pin D7 with internal pullup enabled (INPUT_PULLUP).

⚠ CRITICAL 220V MAINS SAFETY RULES:

  • Unplug Before Touching: Always disconnect the 220V wall plug from the outlet before touching any relay terminal screws or motor wires!
  • Insulate Exposed Wires: The connection between the 220V Neutral wire and the hopper Blue wire carries live return current. Wrap it thoroughly with electrical tape or a screw-on wire nut.
  • No Metal Contact Under Relay: Keep the bottom of the 5V relay module resting on wood, plastic, or thick cardboard so the 220V solder traces cannot touch any metal chassis or breadboard pins.

Step-by-Step Automated Payout Testing Procedure
1

Verify 5V DC Sensor Connections

Ensure Sensor Pin 1 (VCC) is connected to the 5V rail, Pin 3 (GND) to GND rail, and Pin 2 (SIG) to Arduino Pin D7. Slide switch on board is set to BIG.

2

Secure 220V Screw Terminals

With the 220V plug UNPLUGGED, screw the AC Live wire into Relay COM and the Hopper Brown wire into Relay NO. Join AC Neutral to Hopper Blue with electrical tape.

3

Load Coins & Plug In 220V Cord

Place 10 to 15 clean ₱1 coins into the hopper bowl. Place a catch tray under the chute. Now plug the 220V cord into the wall outlet. Motor stays OFF because Relay is inactive.

4

Trigger Payout via Serial Monitor

Open Serial Monitor at 115200 baud. Send 1 to dispense 3 coins. The relay clicks ON, motor spins, dispenses coins, counts live, and clicks OFF instantly at 3!
