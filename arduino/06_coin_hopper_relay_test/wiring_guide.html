12V Coin Hopper & 5V Relay Hardware Specifications & Wire Identification
Red Wire (Power)
+12V Motor Input
Connects to Relay Normally Open (NO) contact. Draws ~0.8A running, up to 1.8A stall.
Black Wire (GND)
Common Star GND
Connects to PSU V- and Arduino GND. Provides common return path for motor and optical sensor.
White/Yellow Wire (Pulse)
12V Coin Pulse Train
Optical counter switch pulls LOW as each coin exits. MUST connect to 10k/4.7k divider!
5V Relay Module
Active LOW Driver
Coil on 5V, Trigger on D8. COM goes to +12V PSU, NO goes to Hopper Red wire. Optocoupler isolated.

Why You MUST Use a Voltage Divider (10kΩ + 4.7kΩ or 5kΩ) for D7:

The 12V Coin Hopper optical sensor output swings between 12V DC (idle) and 0V (coin passing). An Arduino Uno digital pin (ATmega328P) has an absolute maximum rating of 5.5V. Feeding 12V directly into Arduino Pin D7 will instantly destroy the microcontroller's internal input circuitry!

With 5kΩ (2:1 Ratio):
V_out = 12V × [ 5k / (10k + 5k) ] = 12V × (1/3) = 4.00V DC
With 4.7kΩ (Standard):
V_out = 12V × [ 4.7k / (10k + 4.7k) ] = 12V × 0.3197 = 3.84V DC

Both 4.00V (with 5kΩ) and 3.84V (with 4.7kΩ) provide a solid, noise-immune logic HIGH (> 3.0V threshold) while staying comfortably below the 5.5V maximum limit.

⚠ CRITICAL CHASSIS & MOTOR ISOLATION RULES:

  • Relay Flyback Spikes: The hopper DC motor generates high inductive voltage kickback when de-energized. Ensure your 5V relay module contains an active flyback clamping diode and optocoupler isolation.
  • Gravity Exit Chute Angle: Mount the hopper with at least a 30° to 45° downward chute angle to ensure coins clear the optical exit eye immediately without stacking or jamming.
  • 60ms Interrupt Debouncing: The firmware incorporates a 60 ms software lockout in countCoinPulse() to prevent double-counting caused by mechanical coin bounce.

Step-by-Step Bench Testing & Calibration Procedure
1

Multimeter Safety Pre-Check

Before connecting jumper wire to Arduino Pin D7, turn on the 12V PSU. Measure with your digital multimeter between the resistor divider junction and GND. Confirm it reads between 3.7V and 4.1V DC (~3.84V with 4.7kΩ, or 4.00V with 5kΩ). Never allow raw 12V into Pin D7!

2

Verify Relay Clicking

Upload 06_coin_hopper_relay_test.ino. Upon boot, Pin D8 initializes HIGH (Relay OFF). When a payout command is triggered, D8 drives LOW, the relay LED lights up, and you will hear a distinct mechanical click as 12V connects to the motor.

3

Load Clean ₱1 Coins

Place 15 to 25 clean ₱1 Philippine coins into the hopper bowl. Ensure no bent coins, tape, or debris are present. Position a cup or catch tray directly under the coin exit chute.

4

Trigger Test Payouts via Serial

Open Serial Monitor at 115200 baud. Type 1 and Enter to dispense 3 coins (290 ML quota = ₱3.00). Type 2 and Enter to dispense 20 coins (1.5L quota = ₱20.00). Verify exact coin counts!
