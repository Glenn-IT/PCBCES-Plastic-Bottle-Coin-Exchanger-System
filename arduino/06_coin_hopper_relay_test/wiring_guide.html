  • D8 Relay Driver: Active LOW switches 12V DC power to the hopper motor.
  • D7 Optical Pulse Interrupt: Falling-edge interrupt scaled via 10kΩ/4.7kΩ (or 5kΩ) voltage divider (~3.84V–4.00V). 1 pulse = 1 coin dispensed.
  • Testing: Open Serial Monitor at 115200 baud. Send 1 to dispense 3 coins, or 2 to dispense 20 coins.
