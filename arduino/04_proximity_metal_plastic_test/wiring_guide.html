LJ12A3-4-Z/BX Hardware Specifications & Wire Color Identification
Brown Wire (BN)
+12V DC Positive
Connects directly to the 12V 10A PSU rail. Operates from 6V to 36V DC.
Blue Wire (BU)
GND Negative
Connects to Common Star Ground (shared with Arduino GND and 12V PSU -).
Black Wire (BK)
NO Signal Output
NPN Normally Open. Pulls to GND when metal is detected. Connects to 10k/4.7k divider!
Sensing Distance
4 mm Rated
4 mm for steel/iron; ~1.5 mm to 2.0 mm for aluminum soda cans. High-frequency eddy current field.

Why You MUST Use a Voltage Divider (10kΩ + 4.7kΩ or 5kΩ):

The LJ12A3 sensor operates on 12V DC. When no metal is present, its internal pullup outputs 12V. An Arduino Uno digital pin (ATmega328P) has an absolute maximum rating of 5.5V. Feeding 12V directly into Arduino Pin D6 will instantly destroy the Arduino microcontroller!

With 5kΩ (2:1 Ratio):
V_out = 12V × [ 5k / (10k + 5k) ] = 12V × (1/3) = 4.00V DC
With 4.7kΩ (Standard):
V_out = 12V × [ 4.7k / (10k + 4.7k) ] = 12V × 0.3197 = 3.84V DC

Both 4.00V (with 5kΩ) and 3.84V (with 4.7kΩ) are well above the Arduino logic HIGH threshold (3.0V) and 100% safe below the 5.5V maximum limit!

⚠ CRITICAL CHASSIS MOUNTING WARNING:

  • Do NOT mount the LJ12A3 directly into a steel chassis plate! Inductive sensors detect all ferrous and conductive metals. Screwing the threaded barrel into a steel wall will cause the sensor to stay PERMANENTLY TRIGGERED.
  • Always use an acrylic plate, 3D-printed PETG collar, or wooden chute bracket with at least 15 mm radial clearance from metallic cabinet framing.

Step-by-Step Bench Testing & Calibration Procedure
1

Multimeter Safety Pre-Check

Before plugging the divider output into Arduino Pin D6, turn on the 12V PSU and measure between the center tap and GND using a Digital Multimeter. Confirm it reads between 3.7V and 4.1V DC (~3.84V with 4.7kΩ, or 4.00V with 5kΩ). If it reads 12V, check your ground connection immediately!

2

Verify Rear Indicator LED

Bring a steel key, coin, or screwdriver within 4 mm of the blue plastic sensor head. Observe the red LED on the back of the LJ12A3. It should light up brightly when metal is close and extinguish when moved away.

3

Upload Sketch & Open Monitor

Open 04_proximity_metal_plastic_test.ino in Arduino IDE. Set baud rate to 115200 baud. Watch the live serial stream update from [READY ] to [REJECT] as you present objects.

4

Calibrate 2–3 mm Chute Air Gap

Test with a real Coca-Cola/Sprite aluminum can vs a 1.5L and Mismo plastic bottle. Because aluminum has lower magnetic permeability than iron, position the sensor face so inserted cans pass within 2 mm to 3 mm of the blue face.
