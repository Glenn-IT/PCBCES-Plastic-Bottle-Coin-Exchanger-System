⚠️ CRITICAL HARDWARE NOTICE: Do NOT Power SIM900A from Arduino Uno 5V Pin!

The Arduino Uno onboard 5V linear regulator can only supply up to ~400mA before thermal throttling or browning out. The SIM900A RF power amplifier draws burst currents up to 2.0 Amperes during cellular transmission. If connected to the Uno 5V pin, the Uno will continuously reset and the SIM card will fail to register.

  • Power the SIM900A VCC pin directly from the LM2596 5V Buck Converter rail.
  • Connect a 1000µF to 2200µF electrolytic buffer capacitor across VCC and GND right at the SIM900A board pins.
  • Ensure the LM2596 output is calibrated with a Digital Multimeter to exactly 5.0V ± 0.1V before wiring.

Step-by-Step Assembly & Calibration Checklist
01

Calibrate 5.0V Buck Rail

Power the LM2596 module with 12V from the S-120-12 PSU. Turn the brass multi-turn potentiometer on the buck converter until your DMM reads exactly 5.0V DC across OUT+ and OUT-. Turn off PSU before continuing.

02

Solder Buffer Capacitor

Place a 1000µF or 2200µF 16V electrolytic capacitor directly across the SIM900A VCC and GND pins. Respect polarity: negative stripe − connects to GND; long leg + connects to VCC.

03

Wire 5VT & 5VR Serial Lines

Connect SIM900A 5VT to Arduino D11 (SoftwareSerial RX). Connect SIM900A 5VR to Arduino A3 (SoftwareSerial TX). Do not use 3VT/3VR for Uno 5V TTL logic.

04

Prepare & Insert SIM Card

Insert a Standard or Mini-SIM (Smart, Globe, TNT, or TM) into the SIM900A slot with the notched corner aligned as labeled on the chassis. Verify beforehand that the SIM has active airtime credit and PIN lock is disabled.

05

Attach External GSM Antenna

Screw the SMA duck/stub antenna tightly onto the gold antenna jack. If building into a metallic machine enclosure, route the antenna cable outside the cabinet to prevent the metal walls acting as a Faraday cage.

06

Verify Network Registration

Power on the machine. Watch the onboard NET LED: it blinks rapidly (every 800ms) while searching. Once successfully connected to the cell tower, it slows down to a steady blink once every 3 seconds.

Serial Monitor Diagnostic Commands & Test Suite

Upload 07_sim800l_gsm_sms_test.ino to your Arduino Uno and open the Serial Monitor at 115200 baud with Both NL & CR selected. The sketch automatically sends AT synchronizations and offers the following keystroke commands:

1 AT+CMGF=1 & AT+CMGS +CMGS: <idx> OK Transmits test SMS specifically to Admin Phone 1 (09634299114 / +639634299114).
2 AT+CMGF=1 & AT+CMGS +CMGS: <idx> OK Transmits test SMS specifically to Admin Phone 2 (09242074903 / +639242074903).
s AT+CSQ +CSQ: 18,0 OK Cellular signal strength. Value > 12 indicates good transmission quality.

o AT+COPS? +COPS: 0,0,"SMART",0 OK Displays active cellular carrier name (Smart, Globe, etc.).

💡 Direct Passthrough Mode

You can type any standard AT command directly into the Serial Monitor input box (e.g. ATI for firmware revision, AT+CBC for supply voltage, or AT&W to save settings) and view the raw cellular modem output in real time.
