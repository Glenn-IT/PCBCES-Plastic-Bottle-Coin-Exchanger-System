✅ Can I use my 1000µF 50V Electrolytic Capacitor? YES, 100% PERFECT!

A 1000µF 50V capacitor is completely compatible and safe for your 5V servo power rail. Here is why:

  • 1000µF Capacitance: This is the exact reservoir size needed to store charge and instantly feed the servo's 2.5A startup spikes, completely eliminating voltage dips (brownouts) that would cause the Arduino Uno to reboot.
  • 50V Voltage Rating: The 50V printed on the capacitor is its maximum breakdown limit. When used on a 5.0V rail, it operates with a 10x safety headroom. It will run cool, experience zero electrical stress, and last virtually forever! (Any rating ≥ 10V, 16V, 25V, 35V, or 50V works identically).
  • POLARITY CHECK: Ensure the negative leg (shorter leg with white stripe and minus − signs) connects to GND, and the positive leg (longer leg) connects to +5V.

⚠ CRITICAL CHASSIS MECHANICAL WARNING:

  • Provide a mechanical resting ledge under the flap at 0°! A full 1.5L bottle filled with liquid weighs ~1.5 kg. Never allow that entire weight to hang solely on the servo internal plastic/metal spline. A small physical shelf or stop beneath the flap will absorb the impact force and protect your servo gears forever.
  • Never power the servo from Arduino 5V pin! The onboard Arduino 5V regulator can only provide ~400mA, but the MG996R pulls up to 2.5A during sudden moves. Powering it from the Arduino will instantly brown out and reboot the microcontroller.

Step-by-Step Bench Testing Procedure
1

Calibrate 5.0V Buck Rail

Turn on the 12V PSU. Using your Digital Multimeter, measure the output of your LM2596 buck converter. Turn the gold trimmer screw until the output reads exactly 5.00V ± 0.1V.

2

Install 1000µF 50V Capacitor

Insert the 1000µF 50V capacitor directly across the breadboard 5V and GND power rails right next to the servo connection. Verify the white stripe side with minus − signs is plugged into GND!

3

Upload Sketch & Align Horn

Upload 05_mg996r_servo_trapdoor_test.ino to Arduino Uno. When it powers up, the servo writes 0°. Press the servo horn onto the spline so the flap is 100% horizontal flat, then tighten the center retaining screw.

4

Interactive Serial Testing

Open Serial Monitor at 115200 baud. Type 1 to test 90° Accept Drop, 2 to test 180° Reject, 0 to return to Standby, or a to run the automated cycle!
