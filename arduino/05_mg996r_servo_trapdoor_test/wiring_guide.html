Critical Servo Power Guidelines

Brownout Warning: Never power MG996R from the Arduino 5V Pin!

The MG996R is an industrial-grade metal gear servo that draws up to 1.5A to 2.5A peak stall current when opening or closing. The onboard Arduino 5V regulator can only output ~400mA. Attempting to power this servo from the Arduino pin will cause the board to reboot spontaneously!

  • Always connect the Servo Red (+V) wire to the LM2596 5.0V output rail.
  • Connect the 1000µF 16V capacitor in parallel directly across the 5V and GND bus where the servo is plugged in.
