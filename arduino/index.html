12V to 5V Safe Resistor Divider Circuit

The 12V inductive proximity switch (LJ12A3) and the 12V Coin Hopper optical pulse wire produce 12V output signals. To protect the Arduino ATmega328P pins (maximum 5.5V), two identical 10kΩ / 4.7kΩ or 5kΩ voltage divider pairs scale the voltage down to a safe ~3.8V to 4.0V logic level.

Sensor Signal Wire (12V High) ───[ 10kΩ Resistor ]───┬───> Arduino Digital Pin (~3.8V - 4.0V Safe) │ [ 4.7kΩ or 5kΩ Resistor ] │ Common Star GND (0V) With 5kΩ: V_out = 12V × (5kΩ / (10kΩ + 5kΩ)) = 12V × (1/3) = 4.00V DC (Safe 5V Logic) With 4.7kΩ: V_out = 12V × (4.7kΩ / (10kΩ + 4.7kΩ)) = 12V × 0.3197 = 3.84V DC (Safe 5V Logic)
